Subject: Article proposal: on-device AI and encrypted RAID 1 storage on a Raspberry Pi 5 (Embedded & AI issue)

Dear editors,

I'd like to propose an article for the March–April 2027 "Embedded & AI" issue: how Off The Cloud, an open-source (AGPL) home photo server I develop, runs image tagging (RAM++) and face recognition (YuNet and SFace) on a Raspberry Pi 5's CPU. It does this next to encrypted storage (Argon2id and AES-GCM) on two microSD cards mirrored as RAID 1, whose state two bi-colour LEDs on the GPIO show.

The article would cover:
- fitting the ONNX models into 8 GB, and what a 4 GB board can't do;
- scheduling analysis so uploads stay fast;
- the LED driver that follows mdadm's states;
- what encryption at rest costs on the Pi.

About 2,000 words with photos, wiring and performance figures.
