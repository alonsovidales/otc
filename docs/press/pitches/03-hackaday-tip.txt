Subject: RAID 1 on two microSD cards, on-device AI and status LEDs: a Pi 5 photo server

Hi Hackaday,

Off The Cloud is my open-source take on a Google Photos replacement that lives on a Raspberry Pi 5. Some of the bits that might interest you:

- Storage is two microSD cards in USB readers, mirrored with mdadm as RAID 1. Two bi-colour LEDs on the GPIO show each card's state: red when one dies, flashing while the replacement rebuilds. The alert names the USB port to swap.
- Photo tagging (RAM++) and face recognition (YuNet and SFace) run on the Pi 5's CPU. Nothing leaves the box to be analysed.
- Files are encrypted at rest with AES-GCM, under a key unwrapped from the owner's password with Argon2id; the password is never stored.
- The device dials out to a relay, so there's no port forwarding. Friends' devices fetch each other's posts through it, a tiny social network where no server keeps the photos.
