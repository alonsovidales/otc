Subject: Off The Cloud: an open-source Google Photos replacement that runs on a Raspberry Pi 5

Off The Cloud turns a Raspberry Pi 5 into a home server for a family's photos and files:

- full-resolution backup from Android (Google Play) and iPhone (app in review);
- tagging and face recognition on the Pi itself;
- encryption at rest;
- two microSD cards or USB disks mirrored as RAID 1, with GPIO status lights.

Setup needs no terminal (desktop app plus Bluetooth), and remote access goes through an optional relay you can also self-host. It's AGPL-3.0.

Live demo: https://demo.off-the.cloud
