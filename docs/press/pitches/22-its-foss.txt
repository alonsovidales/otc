Off The Cloud (AGPL-3.0) is a self-hosted replacement for Google Photos and iCloud.

- It runs on a Raspberry Pi 5, or any 64-bit Debian or Ubuntu machine.
- Phones back up in full resolution; tagging and face recognition run on the device.
- Files are encrypted at rest and mirrored as RAID 1.
- The Linux sync client has a tray icon and a command line, and can run as a service.

There's a live demo that needs no sign-up: https://demo.off-the.cloud
