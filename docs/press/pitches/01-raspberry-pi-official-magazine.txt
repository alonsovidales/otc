Subject: Article idea: a Raspberry Pi 5 that replaces Google Photos for a family

Hi,

I'd like to propose an article about Off The Cloud, an open-source project I've built that turns a Raspberry Pi 5 into a family's photo server. Phones back up in full resolution, photo tagging and face recognition run on the Pi itself, and files are encrypted at rest on two microSD cards in USB readers, mirrored as RAID 1, with two GPIO status lights showing each card's health. It's set up without a terminal, over Bluetooth from a phone or computer, and there's a live demo at https://demo.off-the.cloud.

It could work as a Project Showcase, or as a tutorial I'd write: the parts, wiring the status lights, flashing the image and setting it up. I can supply high-resolution photos of the build.
