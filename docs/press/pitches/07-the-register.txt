Subject: An AGPL, cloud-free Google Photos replacement on a Raspberry Pi 5, from a solo European dev

Hi Liam,

Off The Cloud is a free software project (AGPL-3.0) I've been building since August 2025. It puts a family's photos, videos and files on a Raspberry Pi 5 at home instead of in Google's or Apple's cloud.

- Phones back up in full resolution.
- Tagging and face recognition run on the Pi, not in a data centre.
- Files are encrypted at rest under a key only the owner's password unlocks: there's no reset and no back door.
- Storage is two mirrored cards or disks.

The relay for remote access is AGPL too, and can be run on your own domain. It's free for two years, then optional at €9.99 a year. There's also a small friends-only feed, device to device, with no ads or algorithm.

The Android app is on Google Play, the Mac app on the Mac App Store, Windows and Linux apps are on the site, and the iPhone app is in review. You can poke at a live, read-only demo in a browser: https://demo.off-the.cloud.
