Name: Off The Cloud
Website: https://off-the.cloud
License: Open source (AGPL-3.0); free. An optional paid relay for remote access.
Platforms: Self-hosted (Raspberry Pi 5, Linux), Android, Mac, Windows, Linux, web
Alternative to: Google Photos, iCloud Photos, Immich, Synology Photos, PhotoPrism, Ente

Description:
Off The Cloud keeps a family's photos, videos and files on a small server at home, a Raspberry Pi 5 or any Debian or Ubuntu machine, instead of in the cloud.
- Phones back up in full resolution in the background.
- Photos are tagged, grouped by person and searchable, with the AI running on the device itself.
- Files are encrypted at rest and stored on two mirrored cards or disks (RAID 1).
- Apps for Android, Mac, Windows and Linux keep folders in sync, and a web app shows everything in a browser.
- An optional friends-only feed shares posts between people's own devices, with no ads or algorithm.
There's a live demo: https://demo.off-the.cloud

Features to tick: Self-hosted, Photo backup, Face recognition, AI-powered search, Encrypted, Privacy focused, Family sharing, RAID, Ad-free
