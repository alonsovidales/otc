Your device and your data

The device runs in your home, on hardware you chose and look after: the Raspberry Pi, its SD card and disks, power and network. You decide what is stored on it, who can reach it, and what it syncs with. That means:

  • Keep backups of anything you can't afford to lose. The two mirrored disks (RAID 1) protect against one disk failing. They don't protect against both failing, the device being lost, stolen or damaged, files being deleted or overwritten, or mistakes.
  • Your device password can't be recovered. Everything on the device is encrypted with a key made from it. If the password is lost, the data can't be read, by you or by us.
  • Syncing works both ways. A folder kept in sync with the device sends deletions and changes in both directions. Check what you connect, and to which device.
  • Handle the device with care. Use the recommended hardware and a proper power supply, don't unplug it while it updates or writes, and don't modify its system unless you know what you are doing.
