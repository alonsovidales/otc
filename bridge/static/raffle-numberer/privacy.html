Privacy

Raffle Numberer's privacy policy. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Raffle Numberer doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

Your ticket designs, numbers and PDFs stay on your Mac. The app has no network access, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

It keeps only its own settings (such as your last design and where its numbers go) on your Mac.

Questions: info@off-the.cloud.
