Raffle Numberer

Raffle Numberer is a Mac app that prints numbers on your raffle ticket design and lays the tickets out on A4 pages, ready to print and cut. It's free on the Mac App Store, in English and Spanish.

Getting started

  1. Open your ticket design, an image or a PDF.
  2. Drag a box over each spot where the number goes, for example on the ticket and on its stub.
  3. Choose the numbers (in order or at random), the font and the color.
  4. Press ⌘E to make the A4 PDF, then print it at 100%.
